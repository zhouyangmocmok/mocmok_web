儿童安全标准

最后更新：2026-10-04

MocMok 是一款面向 18 岁以上成年人的社交与游戏应用。我们对任何形式的儿童性虐待与剥削（CSAE）零容忍。本页说明我们的标准、你如何举报，以及我们如何处置。

禁止的内容与行为

在 MocMok 上严禁：任何儿童性虐待材料（CSAM）；以性方式描绘、谈论或诱导未成年人；为接触未成年人进行的诱骗、勾搭或性勒索；向未成年人发送或索取露骨内容；人口贩运或以未成年人为对象的任何剥削行为；以及与上述内容相关的链接、代号或引流。无论内容是真实、合成、绘制还是文字描述，一律适用。

年龄要求

注册必须填写出生日期，未满 18 岁无法创建账号。如果我们发现账号持有人未满 18 岁，会立即封禁并删除其资料。如果你认为某个用户未成年，请举报。

如何举报

在 App 内，任何用户的头像面板、聊天会话和对局结算页都有「举报」入口，举报理由中请选择「涉及未成年人」，可附带截图。你也可以直接发邮件到 support@mocmok.com，标题注明「Child Safety」。你无需是当事人也可以举报，举报人身份对被举报方保密。

我们如何处置

标记为「涉及未成年人」的举报由专人优先处理，24 小时内核实。一经确认：立即删除相关内容、永久封禁账号并封禁关联设备；保留必要证据并依法向美国国家失踪与受虐儿童中心（NCMEC）CyberTipline 及相关执法机关报告；对于正在发生的危险，我们会配合执法部门紧急响应。涉及 CSAE 的账号不接受申诉。

遵守法律

我们遵守美国联邦法律（含 18 U.S.C. § 2258A 的报告义务）以及我们运营所在地区关于儿童安全的法律，并配合执法机关的合法调查请求。

儿童安全联系人

儿童安全事务负责人：Yang Zhou（MocMok, Inc.）。邮箱：support@mocmok.com。执法机关及儿童保护组织可通过该邮箱直接联系我们。

Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 2026-10-04

MocMok is a social and gaming app for adults aged 18 and over. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. This page explains our standards, how to report, and how we respond.

Prohibited content and conduct

The following are strictly prohibited on MocMok: any child sexual abuse material (CSAM); sexualized depictions or discussion of minors; grooming, solicitation or sextortion aimed at minors; sending explicit content to, or requesting it from, a minor; trafficking or any other exploitation of minors; and links, code words or redirection to any of the above. This applies whether the content is real, synthetic, drawn or written.

Age requirement

A date of birth is required at sign-up and anyone under 18 cannot create an account. If we learn that an account holder is under 18, we ban the account and delete the profile. If you believe a user is a minor, please report them.

How to report

In the app, every user's avatar panel, chat conversation and game results screen has a Report option. Choose the reason "Involves a minor" and attach screenshots if you have them. You can also email support@mocmok.com with "Child Safety" in the subject line. You do not need to be personally involved to report, and the reporter's identity is never shared with the reported user.

How we respond

Reports flagged "Involves a minor" are reviewed by a person with priority, within 24 hours. Once confirmed we immediately remove the content, permanently ban the account and its associated devices, preserve the necessary evidence, and report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline and relevant law enforcement as required by law. Where there is an imminent risk to a child we cooperate with law enforcement on an emergency basis. There is no appeal for CSAE violations.

Compliance with the law

We comply with U.S. federal law, including the reporting obligations under 18 U.S.C. § 2258A, and with child safety laws in the regions where we operate, and we respond to lawful requests from law enforcement.

Child safety point of contact

Designated child safety contact: Yang Zhou, MocMok, Inc. Email: support@mocmok.com. Law enforcement and child protection organizations may contact us directly at this address.
