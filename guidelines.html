社区规范

最后更新：2026-09-07

友善

把对面当成真人。不辱骂、不威胁、不骚扰，也别在游戏里因为输赢人身攻击。

零容忍

色情与露骨内容、仇恨言论、涉及未成年人的不当内容、诈骗引流、毒品武器等违法内容，一经发现立即删除并封禁。

别冒充、别造谣

不冒充他人或官方，不传播虚假信息，不擅自公开他人隐私。不得利用 Agent Match、聊天或其他功能骚扰他人、批量引流、诈骗或规避平台安全机制。

工具在你手里

遇到不舒服的人：结算页或头像面板可以举报，也可以直接拉黑。拉黑后不会再匹配到同一局，也看不到对方的消息。

我们怎么处理

自动过滤会拦下明显违规的文字和图片；举报会在 24 小时内人工核实，视情节警告、删除内容、临时或永久封禁。

联系我们

对协议、隐私或账号处置有任何疑问，或需要申诉，请发邮件给我们，我们会在 1–3 个工作日内回复。 support@mocmok.com

Community Guidelines

Last updated: 2026-09-07

Be kind

There's a real person on the other side. No insults, threats or harassment — and no personal attacks over a game result.

Zero tolerance

Sexual or explicit content, hate speech, anything inappropriate involving minors, scams and solicitation, and illegal content such as drugs or weapons are removed on sight and lead to a ban.

No impersonation, no misinformation

Don't pretend to be someone else or the MocMok team, don't spread false information, and don't share other people's private details. Do not misuse Agent Match, chat or other features for harassment, mass solicitation, scams or attempts to bypass safety systems.

Tools are in your hands

Someone making you uncomfortable? Report them from the results screen or their avatar panel, or block them outright. Blocked users are never matched with you again and you won't see each other's messages.

How we enforce

Automatic filters stop obviously abusive text and images; reports are reviewed by a person within 24 hours, with warnings, content removal, or temporary or permanent bans depending on severity.

Contact us

Questions about these terms, privacy, or an account action? Want to appeal? Email us and we'll reply within 1–3 business days. support@mocmok.com
