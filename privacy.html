隐私政策

最后更新：2026-09-24

1. 我们收集的信息

账号信息：邮箱或手机号、用户名、出生日期、性别、头像与你自愿填写的资料（兴趣、所在城市、社交偏好、约会资料；约会资料里的族裔、宗教等由你自愿选填）。Agent Match 信息：你主动提供的社交需求、兴趣偏好、匹配条件、搜索范围，以及与 Mr.Moc 对话时输入的内容。使用信息：游戏对局记录与成绩、聊天消息、你发送的语音与图片、好友关系、举报与拉黑记录，以及基本设备信息（机型、系统版本、推送令牌）。位置信息：仅在你启用需要位置的匹配功能并授权后获取，用于位置与距离相关的发现和匹配；你可以随时在系统或应用设置中关闭相关权限。

2. 我们如何使用

提供并改进 Agent Match、游戏、聊天与其他匹配功能；根据你选择提供的社交意图、兴趣、位置、搜索距离及其他条件理解需求并进行推荐；进行内容审核与安全保护（游戏内聊天与上传照片的自动过滤、举报处理、封禁）；发送验证码、你开启的推送通知与必要的服务通知；进行故障排查。我们不会向第三方出售你的个人信息，也不会将其用于广告。

3. 第三方服务

为提供服务，我们使用以下第三方处理数据，它们仅按我们的指示处理：Stream（聊天消息、频道与聊天通知）；Amazon Web Services（服务器、邮件发送与自动图片审核）；Twilio（短信验证码）；OpenAI（Mr.Moc 对话、AI 匹配与匹配总结）；xAI（「谁是 AI」里的 AI 玩家）；阿里云（「你画我猜」的画作判定）；Google Maps Platform（设置位置时的地址搜索与地理编码）；Expo（推送通知投递）；Resend（备用邮件发送）；Apple（Apple 登录，仅接收验签后的身份标识与邮箱；以及应用内购买）。你发给 AI 功能的内容——与 Mr.Moc 的对话、「谁是 AI」里的游戏聊天、「你画我猜」的画作——会被发送给对应的服务商以生成回复；我们不会用它们训练模型。

4. 内容审核

游戏内聊天会经过关键词过滤；头像与相册照片会经过自动图像审核。好友之间的私聊与群聊不做预先审查；当你举报某条消息时，我们会人工核实被举报的对话（包括图片）。被举报的对局聊天记录会被保存 30 天用于核实。审核结果可能导致内容删除或账号封禁；涉及未成年人的内容会依法向有关部门报告。

5. 数据保留与删除

你可以在「设置 → 注销账号」申请删除账号，30 天冷静期结束后我们会删除你的账号、资料、聊天与对局数据，仅保留法律要求或用于防止滥用的最少记录。你也可以随时编辑或删除资料、相册与好友关系。你已发送给他人的消息可能仍保留在对方的会话中。

6. 未成年人

本应用仅面向年满 18 周岁的成年人，不面向未成年人；注册时须提供出生日期，未满 18 周岁无法注册。如发现未成年人信息被误收集，请联系我们删除。

7. 你的权利

你可以访问、更正或删除你的个人信息，撤回位置授权，或注销账号。如需导出数据或行使其他权利，请通过下方邮箱联系我们。

8. 安全

密码经过加密存储，验证码只保存摘要，传输使用加密连接。没有任何系统是绝对安全的，请妥善保管你的凭证。

9. 政策变更

我们可能不时更新本政策，重大变更会在应用内通知。

联系我们

对协议、隐私或账号处置有任何疑问，或需要申诉，请发邮件给我们，我们会在 1–3 个工作日内回复。 support@mocmok.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-24

1. What we collect

Account data: email or phone number, username, date of birth, gender, avatar and the profile details you choose to add (such as interests, city, social preferences and your dating profile, which may include ethnicity or religion if you choose to fill them in). Agent Match data: social intent, interests, matching preferences, search radius and what you type when talking to Mr.Moc. Usage data: game records and scores, chat messages, voice messages and photos you send, friendships, reports and blocks, and basic device information (model, system version, push token). Location: only when you enable location-based matching features and grant permission; it is used for location- and distance-based discovery, and you can disable the permission in your device or app settings at any time.

2. How we use it

To operate and improve Agent Match, games, chat and other matching features; to interpret your requests and surface recommendations using the social intent, interests, location, search distance and other preferences you choose to provide; for content moderation and safety (automatic filtering of in-game chat and uploaded photos, report handling and bans); to send verification codes, the push notifications you have turned on and essential service notices; and for troubleshooting. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Third-party services

We rely on the following processors, which handle data only on our instructions: Stream (chat messages, channels and chat notifications); Amazon Web Services (hosting, email delivery and automated image moderation); Twilio (SMS verification codes); OpenAI (Mr.Moc conversations, AI matching and match summaries); xAI (the AI player in Who is AI); Alibaba Cloud (judging drawings in Draw & Guess); Google Maps Platform (address search and geocoding when you set your location); Expo (push notification delivery); Resend (backup email delivery); and Apple (Sign in with Apple, where we only receive a verified identifier and email, and in-app purchases). What you send to an AI feature — your messages to Mr.Moc, in-game chat in Who is AI, drawings in Draw & Guess — goes to the corresponding provider to generate a response; we do not use it to train models.

4. Content moderation

In-game chat is screened against a keyword filter, and avatars and album photos go through automated image moderation. Direct and group chats between friends are not pre-screened; when you report a message, a person reviews the reported conversation, including any images. Chat logs from reported games are kept for 30 days for review. Moderation may result in content removal or an account ban, and content involving minors is reported to the authorities as the law requires.

5. Retention and deletion

You can request deletion in Settings → Delete Account. After the 30-day grace period we delete your account, profile, chats and game data, keeping only the minimum records required by law or to prevent abuse. You can edit or remove your profile, albums and friendships at any time. Messages you have already sent to other people may remain in their copy of the conversation.

6. Children

The App is intended for adults aged 18 or older and is not directed at minors; a date of birth is required at sign-up and anyone under 18 cannot register. If you believe we have collected data from a minor, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw location permission, or delete your account. To export your data or exercise other rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Security

Passwords are stored hashed, verification codes are stored only as digests, and data is transmitted over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure — please keep your credentials safe.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time and will notify you in the App of material changes.

Contact us

Questions about these terms, privacy, or an account action? Want to appeal? Email us and we'll reply within 1–3 business days. support@mocmok.com
