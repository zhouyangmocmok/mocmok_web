The problem

我们想解决的问题

Finding people is easy. Finding the right person is not.

Most social apps ask you to browse profiles and hope a good conversation follows.

找到人很容易，找到对的人很难。

大多数社交 App 让你刷资料卡，然后指望一段好对话会自己发生。

01

Profiles are low-signal

资料卡信息量太低

A profile says little about whether two people will click in a real conversation.

一张资料卡几乎说明不了两个人聊起来会不会投缘。

02

Filters flatten intent

筛选条件压扁了真实需求

Interests, activities and personal preferences are hard to express through fixed filters.

兴趣、想做的事和个人偏好，很难用固定的筛选项表达出来。

03

Matches create a cold start

匹配之后是冷场

A match still leaves two people staring at an empty chat box.

匹配成功后，两个人面对的仍然是一个空白的聊天框。

MocMok starts with what you want to do and who you want to meet.

MocMok 从「你想做什么、想认识谁」开始。

AI agent match

AI 助手匹配

Mr.Moc turns a specific request into one clear match.

Describe the person or activity you have in mind. MocMok returns one best match, a reason it fits, and an icebreaker.

Mr.Moc 把一句具体的需求，变成一个明确的匹配。

描述你想认识的人或想一起做的事。MocMok 只返回一个最合适的人，附上匹配理由和一句开场白。

Example request

举个例子

“Someone who plays tennis, likes trying new restaurants, and enjoys a thoughtful conversation.”
“想找一个打网球、喜欢探店、也聊得来的人。”

How it works

怎么玩

Play. Match. Connect. Play again.

Games and Mr.Moc feed each other. The more you play, the more MocMok knows about who you click with.

玩，匹配，认识，再来一局。

游戏和 Mr.Moc 互相成就。玩得越多，MocMok 越知道你和谁合拍。

1

Jump into a game

先进一局游戏

Join a live table or a daily solo run. No profile browsing required.

加入一桌实时对局，或者玩一局每日单人游戏。不用先刷资料卡。

2

Tell Mr.Moc who you want to meet

告诉 Mr.Moc 你想认识谁

A date, or a buddy for anything: tennis, dinner, hiking, board games. Say it in your own words.

约会对象，或者任何搭子：网球、吃饭、徒步、桌游。用你自己的话说。

3

Get one match, with a reason

得到一个匹配和理由

Mr.Moc picks one person, explains why, and hands you an icebreaker.

Mr.Moc 只挑一个人，说明为什么，再给你一句开场白。

4

Add a friend and play together

加好友，一起玩

Chat 1:1 or in groups, invite them to a room, and replay whenever you like.

私聊或群聊，邀请对方进房间，随时再来一局。

Safety by design

安全机制

Built for adults. Designed with boundaries.

MocMok is for adults aged 18 or older. Reporting, blocking and content moderation are part of the product, not an afterthought.

只面向成年人，也为边界感而设计。

MocMok 仅向年满 18 周岁的成年人开放。举报、拉黑与内容审核是产品的一部分，而不是事后补丁。

18+ only仅限 18+

You must be at least 18 years old to register or use MocMok.

必须年满 18 周岁才能注册或使用 MocMok。

Report & block举报与拉黑

Report concerning behavior from any game or chat, and block people you do not want to hear from again.

在任何游戏或聊天里都能举报不当行为，并拉黑不想再联系的人。
